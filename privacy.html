Privacy notice

For Homeschool YouTube Scheduler, also referred to as Homeschool Scheduler.

Homeschool Scheduler is used to manage The Homeschool Diaries channel for its owner. Its Google access is used for the following functions:

  • Read selected video files and source-file metadata from Google Drive.
  • Read and update a private Google Sheets upload queue containing video titles, descriptions, file references, approval flags, release times, progress, errors and resulting YouTube video identifiers.
  • Upload approved video files and their metadata to the authorized YouTube channel.
  • Read channel information and non-monetary YouTube Analytics reports for channel management and content planning.

The inspected scheduler stores its YouTube access and refresh tokens in Apps Script User Properties. OAuth client configuration and upload-job state are kept in Script Properties. The queue and selected video files are stored in the owner's Google account. Access to the Apps Script project and its stored configuration should be restricted to trusted operators. Credentials are not part of these public pages.

Google processes data through the Google services used by the scheduler. Where the owner authorizes assistance through Composio, relevant queue data or source files may also be processed by that service. That optional assistance has separate permissions and is distinct from the scheduler's YouTube OAuth connection.

The source inspected for this notice has no automatic retention or deletion schedule. The owner controls retained queue records, source files, script properties, and YouTube uploads. Pausing the Apps Script trigger stops future queue processing, but does not cancel videos already scheduled on YouTube. Scheduled releases must be changed separately in YouTube Studio.

The owner can revoke OAuth access through Google Account connections. Revoking access does not itself delete existing files, queue records or uploaded videos. Those items remain subject to the owner's separate deletion and retention choices and the relevant providers' practices.

Questions may be directed to the support address displayed on the scheduler's Google authorization screen.
