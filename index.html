Homeschool YouTube Scheduler

Homeschool Scheduler is a private tool operated by the owner of The Homeschool Diaries. It helps organize selected videos in Google Drive, review titles and descriptions in a private upload queue, and upload approved entries to the owner's YouTube channel. It can schedule an approved video for a specified release time and read non-monetary YouTube Analytics reports to inform future planning. It is not offered as a public sign-up service.

The tool uses separate Google authorizations for its Drive/Sheets workflow and its YouTube channel connection. Publication is controlled by approval and release-time fields in the queue. The operator should review each finished video and its metadata before approving publication.

Privacy information is available in our privacy notice below.
